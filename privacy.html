Privacy Policy | سياسة الخصوصية

Applies to the apps WEAM FX, Funded Guild, Memory Match and Shape Fit (and the small helper app for mothers). Last updated: November 27, 2026 (planned sign-in section added; not active).

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and they do not send personal data to us. Your settings, language choice, trade sheet, journal and game scores are stored only on your device (local storage). They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Third-party content

WEAM FX and Funded Guild load public data such as prices, news and charts (from TradingView and other services) when you are online. These services receive the usual technical request data from your device, such as your IP address, and may set their own cookies or storage. We send them nothing about you, and their own privacy policies apply. Links in the apps open external sites.

If we add sign-in later

Sign-in is not available today. Nothing described below is active, and nothing is collected today.

Planned: optional member sign-in and usage analytics (Funded Guild and العاشر)

Not active yet. If it launches, it will be optional, the apps will keep working without it, and we will ask for your clear consent (a checkbox) before anything is saved. This is what we plan to collect:

  • Sign-in data: your Google account email and name, or the email you type. A nickname is optional.
  • Your ratings and reviews of firms, shown with a display name only.
  • Usage analytics: clicks on firms and listings, what you search, and how you move around the site and app. We use it to improve the service, spot weak points and make suggestions. It is collected only after you tick the consent box, and you can turn it off any time.

We will not sell your data, share it for advertising or show ads based on it. You will be able to see, export and delete your data and close your account from the app, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Google sign-in is handled by Google and our backend provider (Supabase). We will update this page and ask for your consent before this goes live.

Not financial advice

WEAM FX and Funded Guild give general information only. Trading is risky and you can lose money. Nothing in the apps is financial advice.
