Privacy Policy | سياسة الخصوصية

The apps work fully without an account, with no ads and no analytics. Your trade sheet, journal and language choice are stored only on your device (local storage) and never leave it. Both apps show live price charts (gold first) from TradingView. This is a third-party script that loads from TradingView servers when you are online; we send them nothing about you (TradingView may set its own cookies or storage for the chart; drawings are not saved by us), and TradingView's own privacy policy applies to the chart. Content is informational, not financial advice. Trading carries high risk.

Optional free sign-in (members)

Signing in is optional and free. If you choose to sign in, we collect only: your email address, an optional nickname, and the time and version of your consent. We use them to give you member features and to send you community and news messages, and nothing else. Your trade data is never uploaded. The data is stored with our sign-in provider (Supabase) and is not sold or shared. You can sign out, and delete your account and all this data at any time with the "Delete my data and account" button in the Sign in tab. Every message can be unsubscribed from. This is not financial advice.
