Privacy Policy | سياسة الخصوصية

Applies to the apps WEAM FX, Funded Guild, Memory Match and Shape Fit (and the small helper app for mothers). Last updated: November 27, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and they do not send personal data to us. Your settings, language choice, trade sheet, journal and game scores are stored only on your device (local storage). They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Third-party content

WEAM FX and Funded Guild load public data such as prices, news and charts (from TradingView and other services) when you are online. These services receive the usual technical request data from your device, such as your IP address, and may set their own cookies or storage. We send them nothing about you, and their own privacy policies apply. Links in the apps open external sites.

If we add sign-in later

Sign-in is not available today. If we add an optional sign-in, we will update this policy before it goes live, and the apps will work without it.

Not financial advice

WEAM FX and Funded Guild give general information only. Trading is risky and you can lose money. Nothing in the apps is financial advice.
